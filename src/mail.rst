SPARTA mail list
================

The SPARTA mail list is hosted at
`SourceForge <http://sourceforge.net>`_.  The mail list began in Aug
2018.  SPARTA developers try to respond to posted questions in a
timely manner.

* post to list by sending email to sparta-users at lists.sourceforge.net (no subscription required)
* `SPARTA <https://sourceforge.net/projects/sparta/?source=directory>`_ home page on SourceForge
* `SPARTA mail list <https://sourceforge.net/p/sparta/mailman/?source=navbar>`_ home page on SourceForge
* `SPARTA mail list archives <https://sourceforge.net/p/sparta/mailman/sparta-users>`_ on SourceForge

Ways to use the SPARTA mail list
--------------------------------

* Anyone can post messages to the list by sending an email to
  sparta-users at lists.sourceforge.net.
* Anyone can browse/search previous questions/answers `in the archives <https://sourceforge.net/p/sparta/mailman/sparta-users>`_.
* You do not have to subscribe to the list to post Qs, receive answers
  (to your Qs), or browse/search the archives.
* You do need to subscribe to the list if you want emails for all the
  posts (per-message or in digest form), or to answer Qs yourself.  Feel
  free to sign up and help us out!
* You can subscribe/unsubscribe, change your subscriber options, ofview
  the archives from the `SPARTA mail list home page <https://sourceforge.net/p/sparta/mailman/?source=navbar>`_.
* If you post a message and you are a subscriber, your message will
  appear immediately.  If you are not a subscriber, your message will be
  moderated, which typically takes a day.  Either way, when someone
  replies you will receive that reply as a personal email, and the reply
  will appear on the list in the new thread your message started.
  Posting a message sends email to subscribers immediately, but it can
  take a few hours for postings and replies to show up in the
  SourceForge archive.
