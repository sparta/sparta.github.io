SPARTA Authors and Thanks
=========================

The development of SPARTA has been funded by the `US Department of Energy <https://www.energy.gov>`_ (DOE).

.. _doe: https://www.energy.gov




----------


The chief authors of SPARTA are the following folks at Sandia National
Labs:

* Michael Gallis, magalli at sandia.gov
* `Steve Plimpton <https://sjplimp.github.io>`_, sjplimp at gmail.com
* Stan Moore, stamoor at sandia.gov

.. _sjp: https://sjplimp.github.io



Tim Bartel (Sandia) was the chief author of the 2d `ICARUS DSMC code <https://sjplimp.github.io/dsmc.html>`_, a predecessor to
SPARTA.  Some of the parallelization ideas developed for ICARUS have
also been useful in SPARTA.

Dan Rader and Steve Kempka, both managers at Sandia, provided
enthusiastic support for SPARTA development and its release as
open-source software.

Tim Koehler (Sandia) designed the SPARTA logo shown on the `home page <index.html>`_.
